Projects

Real-Time Speech Translation

Bachelor thesis · in progress

English and Slovak live translation with voice cloning for online meetings: voice activity detection, speech-to-text, machine translation and text-to-speech over a WebSocket stream. Runs on a laptop CPU or a GPU. 38 automated tests.

On a Tesla T4 the full cascade with a cloned voice runs at 0.18 to 0.34 real-time factor, with first translated audio in 1.1 to 2 s. One GPU worker served 12 meeting participants (p95 first audio 2.6 s), degraded at 16 and collapsed at 24. The bottleneck is the single text-to-speech worker, 85 to 90 % of compute.

Slovak speech recognition, 18 sentences, one speaker, CPU (lower WER is better)
